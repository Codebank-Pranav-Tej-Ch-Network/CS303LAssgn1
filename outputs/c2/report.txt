p=1 underfits (a straight line cannot capture the sine shape, so both train and test RMSE are high). p=9 and p=15 drive training RMSE very low, but p=15's test RMSE is much worse than its training RMSE -- classic overfitting from an overly flexible polynomial with only 56 training points.

--- (c) Coefficient profile: best Lasso vs best Ridge ---
Best Lasso (lambda=0.0001) has 8 / 15 coefficients below 0.001 in magnitude (effectively zero).
Lasso produces a sparse coefficient profile -- it zeroes out most high-degree terms and keeps only a few informative ones -- while Ridge shrinks all coefficients smoothly toward zero without setting any of them exactly to zero, so its bar chart shows many small but nonzero bars across every degree.
